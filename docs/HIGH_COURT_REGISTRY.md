# Canonical Registry of Indian High Courts (v1.3.0)

**Date:** 2026-11-24  
**Scope:** Canonical registry of all 25 Indian High Courts, their eCourts identifiers, territorial jurisdictions, official websites, and judgment search portals.

---

## 1. Registry Architecture & Verification Policy

1. **Authoritative Grounding:** All entries are verified against the Ministry of Law and Justice (Government of India) and respective official High Court registries.
2. **Empirical Indexing Invariant:** Courts are tagged as `Fully Verified`, `Partially Indexed`, or `Configured (Pending Ingestion)`. Un-indexed courts are never reported as having zero real-world judgments.
3. **Territorial Jurisdiction Mapping:** Multi-state or Union Territory jurisdictions are explicitly enumerated.
